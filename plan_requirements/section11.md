
# 11. Accessibility Requirements

## 11.1 Accessibility Philosophy

Radio Atlas should be accessible to all operators.

Accessibility should be integrated into the design process rather than treated as a compliance exercise.

Because the application depends heavily on visualization and animation, accessibility requirements shall apply equally to:

* Data
* Motion
* Color
* Interaction

---

## 11.2 WCAG Compliance

### ACCESS-001

The application shall comply with **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** requirements.

---

## 11.3 Keyboard Accessibility

### ACCESS-002

All interactive features shall support keyboard navigation.

Users shall be able to access:

* Menus
* Filters
* Timelines
* Narrative cards
* Interactive maps
* Replay controls

---

## 11.4 Visual Accessibility

### ACCESS-003

Color shall never function as the only means of communication.

Alternative indicators shall include:

* Labels
* Icons
* Patterns
* Shapes
* Motion

---

### ACCESS-004

Text shall maintain sufficient contrast ratios.

---

### ACCESS-005

Users shall be able to resize text without loss of functionality.

---

## 11.5 Motion Accessibility

### ACCESS-006

Animations shall support user preferences.

Users shall be able to:

* Reduce motion
* Disable animations
* Reduce visual effects

The application shall support operating-system-level motion preferences.

---

## 11.6 Screen Reader Support

### ACCESS-007

The application shall support:

* ARIA labels
* Semantic HTML
* Accessible navigation structures

---

### ACCESS-008

Charts and visualizations shall provide descriptive alternatives.

Globe visualizations shall expose textual summaries when possible.

---

## 11.7 Cognitive Accessibility

### ACCESS-009

Complex concepts shall be explained using plain language.

---

### ACCESS-010

AI narratives shall support multiple explanation levels:

* Beginner
* Intermediate
* Advanced

---
