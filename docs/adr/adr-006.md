# ADR-006: Implement a Time-Series Architecture for Historical Replay

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Authors:** SolaraSphere Engineering

---

## Context

Historical Replay is one of Radio Atlas's primary differentiators.

The feature allows users to replay historical radio conditions and observe how propagation changes over time.

Users should be able to visualize:

- Signal activity
- Spot density
- Gray-line movement
- Solar conditions
- Band openings
- Band closures
- Geographic activity
- Anomalous propagation events

The competitive analysis revealed that most existing amateur radio software treats historical data as static tables, exported logs, or traditional charts.

Radio Atlas requires a fundamentally different approach.

Historical data must become an animated, explorable environment.

---

## Decision

Replay Mode shall be implemented as a time-series reconstruction engine.

Historical observations shall be stored as timestamped events.

Replay shall reconstruct visual states from stored observations rather than recording rendered frames.

The system shall use **event-based reconstruction instead of video playback.**

---

## Architectural Principles

Replay data shall be:

- Immutable
- Time-indexed
- Queryable
- Reconstructable
- Provider-independent

The replay engine shall reconstruct historical conditions dynamically.

The system shall never store pre-rendered animations.

## Alternatives Considered

### Option 1: Video Playback

Record animations as videos.

#### Advantages

- Simple playback
- Minimal reconstruction logic

#### Disadvantages

- Extremely large storage requirements
- No interactivity
- No filtering
- No user-controlled exploration

---

### Option 2: Snapshot Storage

Store complete globe snapshots at regular intervals.

#### Advantages

- Faster reconstruction
- Simpler queries

#### Disadvantages

- Significant storage requirements
- Data duplication
- Limited scalability

---

### Option 3: Event-Based Reconstruction (Selected)

Store timestamped events and reconstruct historical states dynamically.

#### Advantages

- Minimal storage requirements
- Full interactivity
- Arbitrary timeline navigation
- Dynamic filtering
- Better scalability

#### Disadvantages

- More complex implementation
- Increased processing requirements

---

## Rationale

The replay engine must support user interactions such as:

- Pause
- Play
- Fast-forward
- Rewind
- Timeline scrubbing
- Layer filtering
- Band filtering
- Geographic filtering

These capabilities are impossible with traditional video playback.

An event-driven model provides maximum flexibility while minimizing storage requirements.

## Database Design

Replay data shall be stored in dedicated historical tables.

```text

historical_solar_conditions
historical_forecasts

```

## Caching Strategy

The replay engine shall implement multiple cache layers.

### Server Cache

Use Redis for:

- Frequently requested time windows
- Precomputed aggregations
- Timeline indexes

---

### Client Cache

Use browser caching for:

- Previously viewed replay windows
- User-selected filters
- Replay state

---

Version 1.0 shall support:

| Time Window | Support |
| --- | --- |
| 1 hour | Yes |
| 6 hours | Yes |
| 24 hours | Yes |
| 7 days | Future |
| 30 days | Future |

## Visual Behaviors

The replay engine shall support the following visual behaviors:

### Spot Density

Regions shall pulse according to activity.

---

### Signal Paths

Paths shall brighten as propagation strengthens.

---

### Openings

Propagation openings shall appear and dissolve.

---

### Terminator

The day/night boundary shall sweep continuously across the globe.

---

### Morphing

Historical conditions shall transition smoothly over time.

---

### Forecast Visualization

Predicted paths shall use a distinct visual treatment.

Examples:

- Dashed lines
- Reduced opacity
- Alternative animation patterns

---

### Anomaly Visualization

Anomalous paths shall flare briefly.

---

## Performance Requirements

### Replay Rendering

The replay engine shall maintain:

- 60 FPS target on desktop
- 30 FPS minimum on mobile

---

### Timeline Navigation

Timeline scrubbing shall update within:

- 250 ms for cached data
- 1 second for uncached data

---

### Query Performance

Replay queries shall return within:

- 2 seconds for a 1-hour window
- 5 seconds for a 24-hour window

---
